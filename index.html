How to play

  • One player sets a secret word; the other guesses letters.
  • A right letter fills in the blanks. A miss builds one part of Robbie — he has 10 parts.
  • Guess the word before Robbie is finished and the guesser scores: 10 points per letter, minus 5 per miss (at least 10).
  • If Robbie gets fully built, he powers on, waves, and shows the word — and the word-setter wins the round!
  • Whole-word guesses: you can try to solve it all at once, but a wrong whole-word guess adds 2 parts at once. Careful!
